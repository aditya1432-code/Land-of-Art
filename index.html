About Us

The Land of Art was founded with a single vision — to be the heartbeat of creativity on campus. We organize events, cultural fests, art exhibitions, design workshops, and interactive sessions that encourage imagination and expression. Every year, The Land of Art becomes a home for hundreds of passionate students who wish to express themselves through art and innovation.

Creative Division

Responsible for designs, art direction, and content creation. From poster art to stage setups — creativity starts here.

Technical Division

Developers, tech leads, and engineers who make everything run smoothly — from websites to event systems.

Media & PR

Handles photography, videography, social media presence, and documentation of all events and moments.

Management & Coordination

Our planners and executors — the backbone that keeps The Land of Art operating with precision and flair.

Our Events

  • Land of Art Night: An evening of music, dance, and live performances.
  • Tech & Art Fusion: A unique collaboration between art and technology.
  • Workshops: Design, photography, theatre, and creative writing sessions throughout the year.
